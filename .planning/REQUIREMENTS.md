# Requirements - Bageshree Home Stay Website

## 1. Functional Requirements
- **FR-1**: Responsive Navigation Bar with quick contact, brand leaf insignia, navigation links, and floating/sticky CTA.
- **FR-2**: Cinematic Hero Section with smooth background slideshow, tagline, Konkan highlights, and dual CTAs (Rooms & WhatsApp direct booking).
- **FR-3**: About Section highlighting Konkan culture, mango/cashew orchards, MTDC-approved peace, family warmth.
- **FR-4**: Rooms & Accommodation Showcase with interactive room cards (AC Deluxe, Non-AC Family Garden Room, Spacious Suites), high-res imagery, feature badges (Air Conditioning, Inverter Backup, Clean Bathrooms with Hot Water, Garden views, Plush Beds), and direct room booking query trigger.
- **FR-5**: Amenities & Konkan Experiences (Homely fresh food & breakfast - Pohe, Modak, Konkani seafood/veg thali; Secure compound parking; Ambient night garden lighting; Expansive mango/cashew garden; 24-hr pure water & Wi-Fi).
- **FR-6**: Photo Gallery with category filtering (All, Rooms, Garden & Flora, Night Ambiance, Dining) and full-screen Lightbox zoom modal with keyboard navigation.
- **FR-7**: Guhagar & Konkan Attractions Guide with interactive distance cards (Guhagar Beach, Vyadeshwar Temple, Velneshwar, Hedvi Beach, Durga Devi Temple).
- **FR-8**: Interactive Booking & WhatsApp Inquiry Form with dates, guests, room preference, calculating custom estimate and formatting ready-to-send WhatsApp message and phone dialer.
- **FR-9**: Location, Contact & Interactive Google Map embed with address and direct directions link.
- **FR-10**: Rich Footer with MTDC accreditation badge, phone numbers, email, address, operating hours, quick links.

## 2. Non-Functional & Design Requirements
- **NFR-1**: 100% adherence to no-emoji UI rule (Lucide icons only).
- **NFR-2**: Earthy Konkan luxury color palette (forest green, warm sand cream, sunlit amber, jambha terracotta).
- **NFR-3**: Flawless responsive design from 320px mobile to 4K displays.
- **NFR-4**: Smooth scroll animations, glassmorphism cards, micro-interactions with Framer Motion.
- **NFR-5**: Performance-optimized assets, high accessibility standards (WCAG contrast, ARIA labels, semantic markup).
