# Bageshree Home Stay - Website Architecture & Vision

## Overview
**Bageshree Home Stay** is an MTDC-approved premium Konkan nature retreat & homestay located at Kaundhar Kalsur, Satichamal, near Shringartali-Abloli Road, Guhagar, Ratnagiri, Maharashtra 415724 (MTDC Reg No: R760).

The website delivers an ultra-luxurious, bespoke digital experience (₹1 Lakh+ custom design standard) combining the serenity of Konkan mango/cashew groves, coastal calmness, authentic Maharashtrian hospitality, and modern resort comforts.

## Core Identity & Brand Direction
- **Industry**: Luxury Nature Resort & Authentic Konkan Homestay
- **Audience**: Families, nature lovers, peaceful vacationers, coastal travelers, weekend escapists from Mumbai/Pune
- **Brand Personality**: Earthy, serene, hospitable, prestigious, warm, organic luxury
- **Color Palette**:
  - Forest Green & Emerald Konkan Flora (`#173829`, `#1E4D38`, `#0D2319`)
  - Warm Sand & Konkan Earth Cream (`#FAF7F2`, `#F4EFE6`, `#EDE4D3`)
  - Warm Sunlit Amber & Gold Accent (`#D4A359`, `#C49246`, `#E6BA72`)
  - Deep Jambha Red / Terracotta Ochre subtle tones (`#8C3826`, `#B04D38`)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: Editorial serif / refined display (`Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`, `Cormorant Garamond`)
  - Body & UI: Modern humanist sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`)
- **Iconography**: Lucide React crisp, unified stroke icons (strictly NO emojis in professional UI)
- **Key Actions**: Direct WhatsApp booking inquiry, Room explorer, Amenities showcase, Interactive photo gallery modal, Guhagar attraction guide, Instant pricing & query calculator.
